# Research submission

## Sub-features

Authenticated creation, progress, sourced venue and supply proposal, honest incomplete outcomes.

## How to get to it (user POV)

Open / for the structured brief, or /planning for the free-text brief.

## Driving it with the shared browser

On the homepage, fill the occasion, headcount, date, local start/end time, location, budget, cancellation allowance and supplies.
Submit Find venues once and verify /api/planning creation and navigation to /planning/$id.
Check that the saved request retains every entered requirement, including local event time rather than the browser's timezone.
On /planning, use Tell us about your event and submit one complete brief.
Observe /planning/$id, Conversation and Your options.
Record one venue and requested supply, source links, price evidence, pack size and purchased quantity.
Capture status transitions and exact observation times.

## Gotchas

Live research is variable and consumes credits.
Do not retry an uncertain create.
Missing stock or a final venue quote must remain unknown.
An unavailable product should lead to replacement research, not a claim of purchasability.
For the Berlin birthday rehearsal, use 48 guests, USD 900, USD 20 acceptable cancellation loss and candles.
A candidate with known capacity 40 must not be presented as fitting 48 guests.
Do not claim that rejection happened unless the real run encountered and rejected such a candidate.
Keep EUR prices distinct from a USD budget and unknown candle restrictions visible.
The existing loading animation is presentation, not evidence of completed research steps or measured progress.
