# Hackathon prize tracks

Tony supplied the prize-track slide on 2026-11-07.
The information below is transcribed from that slide rather than independently confirmed current rules.

## Main track

The track is described as "Best autonomous company" and goes to the company most likely to scale to millions.
The slide does not specify what "millions" measures.

- First prize is listed as "iPhone 18 Pro / AirPods 5".
- Second prize is listed as "Mac mini / AirPods 5".
- Third prize is a Mac mini.
- Fourth through sixth prizes are Pocket AI notetakers.
- First place also receives a guaranteed YC interview and $50K+ in sponsor credits.

## Sponsor tracks

Mobilerun rewards best use of Mobilerun.
First, second, and third prizes are three, two, and one virtual iPhones, with advertised values of $8,520, $5,760, and $3,000 respectively.
The slide also lists a developer plan per team.

AgentMail rewards best use of AgentMail.
First prize is a $500 gift card and six months of AgentMail free.
Second and third prizes are six months of AgentMail free.

Monid rewards best use of Monid.
First prize is $1,500 in Monid credits and a $1,500 Visa gift card.
Second prize is $1,000 in Monid credits and a $1,000 Visa gift card.
Third prize is $500 in Monid credits and a $500 Visa gift card.

## Implications for this project

The agreed product scope is sufficient to start implementation planning.
The main-track business case still needs evidence of demand beyond a friend and a hypothesis for sustainable revenue while the current service is free.
These are explicitly unvalidated business questions rather than reasons to expand the happy-path demo.
Sponsor-track integrations should do necessary work in the core flow rather than add features solely to display a sponsor.
No primary prize track or sponsor integration has been selected yet.
