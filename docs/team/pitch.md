# Pitch handoff

You own the customer story, presentation, demo script, and evidence of what the product actually does.
Read the [product baseline](../product-spec.md) and [demo acceptance draft](../demo-acceptance.md).

## Story

A host describes an event, reviews a complete plan, and approves the venue and supplies together.
The service does the purchasing work and later coordinates cancellation through the same event.
The product's value is the completed operational work with traceable results.
The conversation is how the customer gives requirements and makes decisions.

Use the friend's event as the pilot only after confirming its actual requirements.
Do not claim the pilot happened, saved money, or produced revenue without evidence.
The service is currently free; transaction volume is not service revenue.
Keep any future business model separate from the demonstrated product.

## Demo sequence

1. Enter the event brief and show any needed follow-up.
2. Show evidence-backed options and the proposed event plan.
3. Approve the named venue and item together at the displayed total.
4. Show customer payment and supplier confirmation evidence.
5. Cancel the event with one confirmation covering both commitments.
6. Show the resulting cancellations and honest refund status.

Prepare a labeled recording or simulated fallback for an unavailable live step.
Own milestone 8 in the [demo plan](demo-plan.md): the rehearsal, the recorded fallback video, and the agreed demo brief.
Frame the demo around autonomy: count the agent's steps in the progress log against the two human steps, approve-and-pay and cancel.
Say plainly which steps were live, Stripe test mode, or simulated.
Keep the live, recorded, and simulated labels visible rather than explaining them only afterward.
Do not describe negotiation, invitations, or hosting as working features.

## Prize fit

Lead with the main autonomous-company outcome.
Use Monid's track when it performs actual useful tool work in the demo.
Use AgentMail's track only if supplier email genuinely contributes to the demonstrated flow.
Include Mobilerun only if a working supplier interaction needs a phone.
Sponsor logos and unused SDK imports are not evidence of an integration.

## Acceptance and coordination

Get exact live or simulated status and evidence from the agent and transaction owners before rehearsing.
Get the final screens and navigation from the frontend owner.
Prepare a short explanation of customer collection versus supplier payment if judges ask.
Describe remaining live prerequisites honestly.
Do not publish or send invitations as part of preparing this draft.
