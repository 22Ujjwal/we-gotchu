# Start with an event purchasing service and a paid pilot

The hackathon scope and approval rules below are revised by [0002](./0002-limit-hackathon-scope-and-require-action-approval.md).

We will begin with purchasing for events rather than general business purchasing.
The intended service covers supplier sourcing, quote comparison, required customer approval, booking, payment, and reconciliation.
This limits the initial scope to one purchase category while we investigate what customers need beyond existing event planning services.

The hackathon target is one completed, paid customer job.
Tony has a friend who can buy the service for his own event.
We will use that actual event to define the first customer job.
We will charge for handling purchasing work, including supplier coordination and payment follow-through after the required customer approval.
The service includes event planning, supplier sourcing, negotiation, and booking with customer confirmation.
Planning covers the event plan and supplier arrangements.
The customer handles invitations and hosting for the first version.
The agent may negotiate within the customer's requirements without seeking approval for every supplier message.
Before committing, it must obtain confirmation of the supplier, deliverables, date, full price, payment schedule, and cancellation terms.
Material changes to approved terms require fresh confirmation.
The agent will discuss the customer's needs during intake rather than require Tony to specify the friend's event during this interview.
Tony wants a future skill to guide that conversation.
No event details have been established yet, and illustrative scenarios are not customer requirements.
The event details, service fee, purchasing authority, and evidence of customer need remain unresolved.
We accept the risk that customer and supplier response times may limit what can finish during the hackathon.
Technology choices remain outside this decision.
