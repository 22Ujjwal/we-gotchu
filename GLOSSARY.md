# Event purchasing

This glossary defines the business terms agreed during the idea interview.

## Language

**Customer**:
The person or organization buying our service to arrange purchases for an event.
_Avoid_: User, attendee

**Supplier**:
A business providing a venue, goods, or services for the customer's event.
_Avoid_: Customer

**Intake**:
A conversation in which the agent and customer establish the event's needs and constraints.
_Avoid_: Booking, approval

**Event plan**:
A proposed combination of a venue and related event purchases, with a combined price and cancellation terms for customer review.
_Avoid_: Booking confirmation

**Booking confirmation**:
The customer's permission for the service to commit to a supplier booking.
_Avoid_: Intake, preference

**Action approval**:
The customer's permission for a specified step that contacts an external person or involves payment.
Approval of an event brief alone is not action approval.

**Service fee**:
An amount charged for our planning and purchasing work, distinct from supplier costs.
The first version charges no service fee.
_Avoid_: Supplier payment, event budget

**Event cancellation**:
One customer request for the service to cancel the event's supplier bookings and purchases under their respective terms.
The request does not itself establish that every supplier has canceled or refunded its transaction.

**Customer pilot**:
A first customer event used to test whether we can deliver planning, purchasing, and cancellation.
The customer pays supplier costs while our service is free.

## Event ledger

The ARRANGE module that stores event facts and checks ownership, supplier-send permission, offer evidence, approved payment amounts and supplier confirmations.
It does not choose suppliers or decide what the agent does next.
